What this policy covers

This policy covers the ProXimos App for Android, which you use to set up ProXimos Hubs and configure ProXimos Nodes and Beacons. It explains what data the app handles, what stays on your phone, and what is sent to HARDWARIO.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Data that stays on your phone

Most of what the app does happens between your phone and your own ProXimos devices. The following is never sent to HARDWARIO or anyone else.

Permission or data What the app uses it for Sent anywhere?
Camera Scanning QR codes on ProXimos devices. Images are decoded on the phone and not stored. No
Location Reading the name of the Wi-Fi network your phone is connected to, to suggest it during Hub setup. Your location is not recorded. No
Bluetooth Connecting to your ProXimos Hub and finding nearby Beacons. Only to your own Hub
NFC Reading and configuring ProXimos Nodes held against the phone. Only to your own Node
Wi-Fi network name and password Connecting your Hub to Wi-Fi. They are sent over an encrypted Bluetooth link to your Hub only. Only to your own Hub
Fingerprint, face or device PIN Optional app lock. Handled by Android; the app only learns whether unlocking succeeded. No
Saved Nodes, their keys, names, tags, templates and change log Letting you manage your devices. Stored on the phone; keys are kept in the Android Keystore. Only when you choose to export or share them

When you export devices or configurations (as a QR code, CSV or JSON file), the file goes wherever you send it, and the recipient's own privacy terms apply.

Data sent to HARDWARIO

The app contacts HARDWARIO only when you claim a Node, and when it later works with a Node you claimed. It talks to ATELOS, HARDWARIO's device service at atelos.hardwario.com. If you only import Nodes (by QR code, file or typed key), nothing is sent to HARDWARIO.

Claiming registers a Node to your phone, so the app can fetch the Node's encryption keys. To do that without asking you to create an account, the app sets up a guest account tied to your phone.

Data When Why
Guest identifier and secret: two values computed from Android's app-specific device ID with a one-way function (HMAC-SHA256). The device ID itself is not sent. First time you claim a Node Identify your phone's guest account, so it keeps owning your Nodes after you reinstall the app
Node serial number and the claim code read from the Node When you claim a Node Register the Node to your guest account
Node serial number After claiming, and when the app refreshes your claimed Nodes Fetch the Node's encryption keys
Record of a configuration change: which settings changed, the time, and an encryption counter When you change the configuration of a Node whose keys come from ATELOS Keep the Node's encryption counter in step, so every message to the Node stays secure

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Why we process it

We use the data above only to provide the claiming and key-management features you ask for. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the legal basis is the performance of our agreement with you to provide the app's features (Article 6(1)(b)). Keeping the encryption counter in step also serves our legitimate interest in keeping your devices secure (Article 6(1)(f)).

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions, and we do not sell it.

Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. ATELOS runs on servers that a hosting provider operates for us in the EU; the provider only stores the data on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes. We may disclose data where the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep the guest account and the records of your claimed Nodes until you ask us to delete them.

  • Release a Node: use Unclaim in the app to hand a Node on. Its link to your guest account is removed.
  • Delete your guest account in the app: first use Unclaim on every Node you claimed. Then open the menu, tap the ProXimos logo at the bottom of the menu five times in quick succession to turn on Debug mode, and choose Delete generated ATELOS account at the top of the menu. The account and its records are deleted, and the app forgets the account on your phone.
  • Or ask us by email: write to ask@hardwario.com with the subject "ProXimos App account deletion". Include the serial number of at least one Node you claimed, so we can find the account. We delete the account and its records within one month and confirm by email.
  • Data on your phone: uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes everything stored on the phone.

Security

All communication with ATELOS uses HTTPS, and the app checks the server's certificate against a copy built into the app, so it cannot be redirected to an impostor server. Communication with Nodes is encrypted with each Node's own key, and the Hub requires an authorised Bluetooth session before its settings can change. Keys stored on the phone are protected by the Android Keystore.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing, or a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Write to ask@hardwario.com. You can also complain to the Czech data-protection authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (uoou.gov.cz), or the authority in your own EU country.

Children

The app is a tool for installing and servicing sensor equipment and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
