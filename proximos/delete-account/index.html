ProXimos App — Delete your account

ProXimos App by HARDWARIO a.s.

When you claim a ProXimos Node, ProXimos App creates an anonymous guest account for your phone in ATELOS, HARDWARIO's device service. There is no username, email or password. You can have this account and its data deleted in either of two ways.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Use Unclaim on every ProXimos Node you claimed, so the Nodes can be claimed again later.
  2. Open the menu and tap the ProXimos logo at the bottom of the menu five times in quick succession. This turns on Debug mode.
  3. Choose Delete generated ATELOS account at the top of the menu and confirm.

Option 2: by email

Write to ask@hardwario.com with the subject "ProXimos App account deletion". Include the serial number of at least one Node you claimed, so we can find the account. You do not need the app installed for this.

What is deleted

  • The guest account and its identifier.
  • The link between the account and every Node it claimed. The Nodes are released, so they can be claimed again.
  • The records of configuration changes made to those Nodes.

Nothing that identifies you or your phone is kept. We complete email requests within one month and confirm by email.

Data stored on your phone (saved Nodes, keys, templates, change log) is not on our servers. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes it.
